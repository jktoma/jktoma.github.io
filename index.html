About Me

I'm looking for a full-time mechanical design role in robotics, automation, or product development, where I can take parts from concept through release and production.

Professional headshot

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Northeastern University graduating in May 2027, with two co-ops in design and manufacturing environments and a strong foundation in SolidWorks, drawing and documentation, and prototyping.

At HighRes Biosolutions, I designed 3D-printed Onyx sensor mount fixtures and adapters for an automated storage system, then drew each part, built the bills of material, and released it through engineering change orders in Bluestar PLM. At Robert Reiser & Co., I modeled product extrusion inserts that were 3D-printed and tested for customers, managed drawings in SolidWorks PDM, and assembled machine components that shipped to customers. Working across design, documentation, and hands-on assembly showed me how much a part's design affects how easily it can be built.

I am currently working with my capstone team to develop an automatic volleyball net tensioner that attaches to existing posts and cable systems, which has involved patent research, material selection, and load analysis.

Outside of class I studied abroad at Yonsei University in Seoul, and I spend my free time rock climbing, playing golf, and learning songs on guitar. I'm looking for mechanical design roles where I can own a part from concept through production.
